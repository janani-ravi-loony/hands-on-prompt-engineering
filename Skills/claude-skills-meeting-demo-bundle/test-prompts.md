# Test Prompts for the Meeting Action Brief Skill

Use these after uploading and enabling the Skill.

## Should trigger

1. Attach `sample-meeting-notes.txt` and ask:
   Turn these notes into a clear post-meeting action brief.

2. Attach `sample-meeting-notes.txt` in a new chat and ask:
   Can you make sense of this meeting and tell me what we actually decided, who owns what, and what could block launch?

3. Paste any meeting transcript and ask:
   Extract the decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, risks, and unresolved questions.

## Should NOT trigger

4. Ask:
   Explain the difference between a meeting agenda and meeting minutes.

5. Ask:
   Draft an agenda for a 30-minute product launch meeting.

6. Ask:
   What are three good ways to keep meetings short?

The point of prompts 4–6 is to show that the Skill is targeted to processing meeting notes/transcripts rather than every question about meetings.
