# Priority Rules

Use these rules only for action items and risks.

## P0 — Critical
Use P0 only when the notes indicate:
- an active security, legal, safety, or severe production incident; or
- a committed launch/release is blocked and action is required within 2 business days.

## P1 — High
Use P1 when the item:
- blocks a committed milestone or launch;
- requires an approval before money can be spent or work can proceed;
- is due within 7 calendar days; or
- resolves a known customer-facing defect before release.

## P2 — Medium
Use P2 when the item:
- is necessary but is not currently blocking a committed milestone;
- supports readiness, documentation, analytics, training, or follow-up work; or
- has a deadline more than 7 days away.

## P3 — Low
Use P3 for optional improvements, backlog items, or explicitly post-launch work that does not block current commitments.

## Guardrails
- Do not assign a higher priority merely because a topic sounds important.
- When evidence is insufficient, use `Priority unclear`.
- A stated decision to defer work until after launch normally makes it P2 or P3 unless the notes identify a separate urgent risk.
