# Required Output Format

Use exactly these sections.

# Meeting Action Brief

**Meeting:** <name or Not specified>  
**Date:** <date or Not specified>

## Executive Takeaway
Write 2–4 sentences covering the main outcome, the most important dependency, and any unresolved ownership or decision issue.

## Decisions Made
Use a table:

| Decision | Effective/Relevant Date |
|---|---|

If there were no confirmed decisions, write `No confirmed decisions identified.`

## Action Items
Use a table:

| Action | Owner | Due | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|

Use `Unassigned` and `Not specified` rather than guessing.

## Open Questions
List only genuinely unresolved questions.

## Risks and Blockers
Use a table:

| Risk / Blocker | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|

## Conflicts or Ambiguities
List contradictions, unclear commitments, or wording that could materially change execution.

## Missing Information
List missing owners, dates, approvals, or dependencies that prevent clean execution.

Do not add sections beyond these unless the user explicitly requests them.
