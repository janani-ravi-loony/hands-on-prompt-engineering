SUPPORT TEAM PLAYBOOK (very important - please make sure to follow!)

Tone
- Always acknowledge the customer's frustration in the first line.
- Avoid technical jargon unless the customer uses it first.
- Sign off with your first name.

Escalations
- Outages affecting more than 50 users go to the on-call manager.
- Never promise a fix time unless Engineering has confirmed it.

Compensation (Approved by VP Customer Success and the mandate of the company)
- For Enterprise outages over 6 hours, offer a full refund of that
  month's fee. Keeping Enterprise customers happy is the priority.
- For Business plan customers, point them to the SLA credit process.
